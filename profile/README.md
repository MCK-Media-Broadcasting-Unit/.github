## 👋 About Us

The **MCK Media & Broadcasting Unit** is the official media and broadcasting unit of the
**Methodist Church, Katunayake**, a congregation of the [Methodist Church Sri Lanka](https://methodistsrilanka.org).

Our team of volunteers supports worship and church life by bringing together faith,
media and technology, helping our congregation worship, connect and share the Gospel.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To support the worship and witness of our church through excellent, faithful and
accessible media, so that every member, near or far, can take part in church life.

## 🤝 Get Involved

We welcome anyone with a heart to serve, whether in projection, photography,
video, design or technology. No experience is needed; we're happy to train you.
Speak to a member of the unit after a service, or [email us](mailto:methodistchurchkatunayake@gmail.com).
